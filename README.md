# Motorola_Sales_Data
This project presents an interactive Motorola Sales Dashboard developed in Power BI to analyze sales performance, revenue trends, product-wise sales, regional performance, and key business metrics.  
The dashboard helps stakeholders monitor sales activities, identify top-performing products, and make data-driven business decisions through dynamic visualizations and KPI tracking. Power BI dashboards are commonly used to analyze sales trends, product performance, and regional insights for better decision-making.

 --> Key Features.
📈 Sales Trend Analysis
💰 Total Revenue & Sales KPIs
📱 Product-wise Sales Performance
🌍 Region-wise Sales Analysis
🏆 Top Performing Products
📅 Time-based Filtering (Month/Quarter/Year)
🎯 Interactive Slicers and Drill-Down Reports
📊 Dynamic Visualizations for Business Insights
